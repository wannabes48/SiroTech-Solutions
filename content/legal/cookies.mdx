## 1. What Are Cookies?
Cookies are small alphanumeric text files stored on your device when you browse websites. They enable the site to remember your preferences (such as language, active navigation state, or currency selection) and provide analytical telemetry to improve site performance.

## 2. Categories of Cookies We Use
We categorize our cookies and client-side storage as follows:

### A. Strictly Necessary Cookies
Essential for the basic functioning and security of the website and client portal.
- **Session Tokens**: Maintain your authenticated session when logging into the Client Portal.
- **CSRF Tokens**: Prevent cross-site request forgery attacks on forms and API submissions.

### B. Functional & Preference Cookies
Allow the website to remember user preferences.
- **Currency Preferences**: Stores your active choice between KES, USD, or CAD so you don't need to reselect on every page visit.
- **Theme Settings**: Remembers visual preferences such as high contrast or reduced motion.

### C. Performance & Analytics Cookies
Gather anonymized aggregate data on how visitors explore our platform, helping us identify broken links and optimize load times.

### D. Marketing & Communications Cookies
Used exclusively for engineering insights recommendations and webinar/event announcements. We do not engage in third-party advertising retargeting networks.

## 3. Managing Cookie Preferences
You can customize or withdraw your consent at any time directly on this website by clicking **"Cookie Preferences"** in the footer of any page, or through the interactive preferences banner. You can also control and disable cookies via your browser settings at any time (e.g. Chrome, Firefox, Safari). Please note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may restrict access to authenticated areas such as the Client Portal.
