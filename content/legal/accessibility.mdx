## 1. Our Commitment
SiroTech Solutions is dedicated to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We continually improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards across all client deliverables and our own digital web properties.

## 2. Standards & Conformance
We target compliance with the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA**. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with visual, auditory, cognitive, and neurological impairments.

### Key Accessibility Features Built Into Our Platform:
- **Semantic HTML**: Proper use of landmarks, section headers, and semantic regions for screen reader navigation.
- **Keyboard Operability**: All interactive controls (navigation pills, accordions, dialogs, carousels) are fully operable via keyboard tabs and arrow keys without keyboard traps.
- **Color Contrast**: Text and visual indicators meet or exceed the minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio against their respective background surfaces.
- **Reduced Motion**: Respects the `prefers-reduced-motion` media query by disabling decorative animations and providing instant rendering.
- **Focus Indicators**: High-visibility focus rings on all interactive elements.

## 3. Feedback & Inquiries
If you encounter any accessibility barriers on our website, please notify us at **accessibility@sirotech.com**. We take all feedback seriously and aim to remediate reported issues within two business days.
