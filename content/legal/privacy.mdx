## 1. Introduction & Overview
SiroTech Solutions ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy and treating personal data with the highest degree of security and confidentiality. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding the collection, processing, storage, and transfer of personal information collected via our website, client portal, APIs, and consulting services.

We comply fully with the **Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (DPA)**, the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) guidelines, and, where applicable to international clients, the European Union **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** and the **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)**.

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect information that you directly provide to us, as well as data gathered automatically when you interact with our platforms:

### A. Information Provided Directly
- **Contact Details**: Name, corporate email address, telephone/WhatsApp number, job title, and organization name submitted through our project scoping forms or consultation booking systems.
- **Project Specifications**: Project brief descriptions, architectural needs, estimated budgets, and technical system specifications.
- **Client Account Data**: Credentials, organization profiles, and billing information for authenticated users accessing our Client Portal.

### B. Automatically Collected Data
- **Technical Information**: IP address, browser type and version, time zone settings, operating system, and platform details.
- **Usage Metrics**: Page interaction information, navigation paths, download errors, and session duration metrics.

### C. Site Assistant (AI Chat Widget)
- **Ephemeral AI Processing**: Queries submitted to our website Site Assistant are processed ephemerally using Claude language models provided by **Anthropic, PBC**. Customer queries are strictly processed to generate real-time answers and UI navigation cards; customer messages are **not** used to train AI models.
- **Transcripts Not Saved by Default**: Conversations are not retained on our servers by default. A transcript is saved only when you explicitly select the *"Attach this chat to my enquiry"* checkbox on an inline contact or project enquiry form. In that case, the transcript is retained solely alongside your lead record in accordance with our standard lead data retention schedule.
- **Browser-Side Storage**: In-progress chat history is stored strictly in your browser's local `sessionStorage` (`sirotech-chat-v1`) to persist conversation state across page navigation. It can be cleared immediately by clicking the Reset button or clearing browser storage.
- **Anonymous Telemetry**: We record anonymous product event counts (such as widget open, message volume count, and card interactions) without message text or identifiable payload data.

## 3. Lawful Basis for Processing
Under Section 30 of the Kenya Data Protection Act and Article 6 of the GDPR, we process personal data under the following legal bases:
- **Contractual Obligation**: Necessary to execute service agreements, deliver software development, or provide architectural proposals.
- **Consent**: Explicit consent granted when subscribing to our technical newsletter or submitting an inquiry.
- **Legal Compliance**: Retaining financial and tax documentation in compliance with the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) and corporate statutory laws.
- **Legitimate Interests**: Improving platform performance, enhancing cybersecurity, and preventing fraudulent transactions.

## 4. Third-Party Sub-Processors & Data Transfer
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share information with vetted technical sub-processors under strict data protection agreements:
- **Cloud Infrastructure & Hosting**: Cloudflare (edge security & CDN), Vercel (application hosting & serverless compute), Neon & Supabase (encrypted cloud database hosting).
- **Artificial Intelligence**: Anthropic, PBC (powers the website Site Assistant; queries processed ephemerally under strict confidentiality with zero model training).
- **Payment Processing**: Paystack Payments Limited (PCI-DSS Level 1 certified gateway handling Visa, Mastercard, and Safaricom M-Pesa).
- **Email & Communications**: Resend, Postmark for transactional system alerts and client notifications.

Cross-border transfers outside Kenya or the EEA are conducted in accordance with statutory standard contractual clauses (SCCs) ensuring an adequate level of data protection.

## 5. Security Measures & Encryption
We enforce defense-in-depth security measures to protect your data:
- TLS 1.3 encryption in transit for all web and API traffic.
- AES-256 encryption at rest for database storage and automated backups.
- Strict role-based access control (RBAC) and multi-factor authentication (MFA) for administrative back-office systems.

## 6. Your Rights
Under the Kenya Data Protection Act and GDPR, you have the following rights:
- **Right to Access**: Request copies of your personal data held by us.
- **Right to Rectification**: Request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")**: Request deletion of personal records where statutory retention obligations do not apply. If you submit a data-deletion request, any chat transcript attached to your lead record is permanently erased from our lead database alongside your lead file.
- **Right to Object / Opt-Out**: Opt-out of marketing communications at any time.

To exercise any of these statutory rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at **privacy@sirotech.com**.
