## 1. Overview
At SiroTech Solutions, we operate on a transparent, milestone-oriented delivery schedule. Because our services involve customized software engineering, architectural design, and dedicated developer hours, this policy governs the terms of refunds and cancellations.

## 2. Project-Based Work
- **Initial Deposit**: The 50% initial commitment deposit covers architectural scoping, schema design, and sprint allocation. Once architectural work has commenced, the deposit is non-refundable.
- **Milestone Rejection**: If deliverables submitted at a milestone do not conform to the documented SOW, the Client has the right to request revisions as outlined in the service agreement before any subsequent milestone billing occurs.
- **Pre-Deployment Final Payment**: The final 50% milestone fee is invoiced upon staging acceptance. Once accepted and deployed to production, project fees are final and non-refundable.

## 3. Monthly Retainer & Maintenance Plans
- **Cancellation**: Monthly support and maintenance retainers can be cancelled at any time by providing fourteen (14) days written notice prior to the next billing cycle.
- **Refunds**: Fees for active billing months already underway are non-refundable as developer capacity and automated infrastructure monitoring are provisioned in advance.

## 4. Contact
For any billing or cancellation questions, please reach out to **billing@sirotech.com**.
